Holiday lighting FAQs

Have a question? Here are a few things to consider before requesting your estimate.

When should I book Christmas light installation?

Reach out early, especially if you have a preferred installation window. Availability depends on the seasonal schedule.

Can I request a custom design?

Yes—tell us whether you prefer classic warm white, colorful holiday lights, roofline lighting, or a display that includes landscaping.

Do you offer removal after the holidays?

Ask about seasonal removal when you request your estimate so the available service options can be confirmed.

What areas do you serve?

We’re based around San Tan Valley and can discuss projects in nearby East Valley communities. Contact us to confirm service availability for your address.
