Santa's Little Helper website package

Included:
- index.html: responsive website
- thank-you.html: form confirmation page

Form:
The estimate request form is configured for Netlify Forms. After deploying the folder/site to Netlify, open the site's Forms section, confirm that the form is detected, and test a submission. Netlify account/plan capabilities and current form limits may vary. If using another host, connect its form handler or backend and update the form action.

Gallery:
The gallery currently shows illustrative design concepts, not actual customer installations. Replace these cards with photos of your real projects when available.

Publish:
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Deploy the extracted folder to a hosting service such as Netlify.
3. Test the estimate form and mobile layout.
4. Connect a domain you own in the hosting dashboard.
5. Check business details, service area, service terms, and any claims before launch.

The site is built but not published; no domain has been registered or connected.
